01 / Overview

Desktop-class rendering.
Now in your pocket.

3D Gaussian Splatting delivers exceptional visual quality, but its sorting overhead and storage footprint make mobile deployment difficult. Mobile-GS removes those constraints without giving up fidelity.

We identify alpha blending—and its costly depth sorting—as the primary computational bottleneck. Our depth-aware order-independent renderer eliminates sorting and renders all Gaussians associated with a target pixel in a single pass.

A neural view-dependent enhancement strategy restores accurate appearance in overlapping geometry, while spherical harmonics distillation, neural vector quantization, and contribution-based pruning dramatically reduce storage.

Key finding
Sorting was the bottleneck.
So we removed it.

Runtime analysis shows that sorting dominates inference cost in standard 3DGS. Removing it unlocks several-fold acceleration, while our learned appearance model preserves visual quality.
