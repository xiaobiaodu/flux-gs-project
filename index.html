01 / Overview

Desktop-class rendering.
Now in your pocket.

High-order spherical harmonics preserve rich view-dependent appearance, but their inference and storage overhead make them difficult to deploy on resource-constrained mobile platforms.

Flux-GS introduces a Monte Carlo Specular Energy Aggregator that samples third-order radiance residuals and gathers visually salient specular energy into a compact latent space. This preserves expressive lighting in lower-order bands without distillation or pre-training.

An Attribute-Conditioned SH Enhancement module recovers high-frequency detail through Gaussian-aware offsets before inference. Multi-view alpha guidance further improves structural consistency while removing redundant primitives.
